Environment layers written 2026-11-20

bathy.asc, disttocoast.asc, blocks.asc: scripts/build_baltic_bathymetry.py --lon 13 22 --lat 53.9 59.5
  --cellsize 1000. EMODnet Bathymetry DTM (WCS emodnet:mean, mean elevation), tiles merged and
  reprojected to EPSG:3035 with average resampling; water = mean elevation at least 5 mm below
  zero (13 shallower cells, written as depth 0.00 in the first build, set to land on 2026-11-21
  and distance to coast recomputed; the model already read them as land); distance to coast =
  Euclidean distance to the nearest non-water cell (m); blocks = 40 km squares. EMODnet Bathymetry
  Consortium, EMODnet Digital Bathymetry (DTM), CC BY 4.0.

salinity01-12.asc: scripts/fetch_copernicus_salinity.py + scripts/build_baltic_environment.py.
  Copernicus Marine BALTICSEA_MULTIYEAR_PHY_003_011 (dataset cmems_mod_bal_phy_my_P1M-m, version
  202303), sea water salinity at the top level (0.5 m), monthly means for 2021: the product and
  year DEPONS used for Kattegat (TRACE 2.6). Bilinear onto the grid, gaps from the nearest value.
  Copernicus Marine Service, free under the Copernicus Marine licence. Checked against DEPONS's
  Kattegat salinity on the overlap near the Sound: January medians 8.53 and 8.53, July 7.31 and
  7.59, correlation 0.97-0.99.

sediment.asc: scripts/build_baltic_environment.py. Median grain size (phi) from EMODnet Geology seabed
  substrate (1:1M, 1:250k, 1:100k, Lithuanian 1:50k multiscale; finer overwrites coarser), CC BY 4.0,
  with DEPONS's per-class values (TRACE Appendix 1, Table S1, as in DEPONS's Kattegat sediment.asc):
  Mud 7.142, sandy Mud 4.40, muddy Sand 3.155, Sand 2.277, Coarse 1.218, Mixed 1.495, Rock -8.
  'Mud to muddy Sand' without a finer class gets 4.40 (sandy Mud; a choice made here). Gaps (lagoons)
  from the nearest value.
