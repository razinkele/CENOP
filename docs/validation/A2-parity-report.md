# A2: CENOP against DEPONS 3.2, quantitative cross-validation

Date: 2026-11-19. Roadmap item A2. Design and bands:
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-a2-cross-validation-design.md`. Full tables and figures,
generated by `python3 -m validation.a2.report`: `A2-parity-report-tables.md` (this is run 2;
run 1 is kept in `run1-before-ship-fix/`).

## Result

- **Undisturbed Kattegat: CENOP agrees with DEPONS** in all three dispersal modes. No metric
  fails. Population differs by at most 4.2% and 1.6 standard errors at every checkpoint,
  mean energy by at most 0.13, the share of animals per 40 km block correlates at 0.999, and the
  age-at-death distributions are indistinguishable (p 0.32 to 0.65).
- **Ships: CENOP does not yet agree with DEPONS.** After a defect found by this comparison
  was fixed, the effect of ships on the year-1 population matches (0.911 against 0.918 of
  the undisturbed population), as do location and age at death. But CENOP animals react to
  ship noise on 28.3% of animal-ticks against 19.2% in DEPONS, and population and energy
  fail at early checkpoints. The cause is open.

large for eight seeds to rule out a difference of the size the band forbids.

## The ship scenario: what happened

**Run 1 failed, and the failure was a real defect.** CENOP animals were exposed to ship noise
on 2.1% of animal-ticks against 19.2%, and ships lowered CENOP's population by under 1%
against 8%. Each route in the Kattegat ship file is one passage in a 721-tick cycle: wait at
the first waypoint, sail, wait at the last. CENOP skipped the first wait, so all 5,148 ships
sailed at once from tick 0, and then steered each ship back to its first waypoint at the last
waypoint's speed, which is 0 in that file. Every ship was stranded after one passage and ship
noise stopped at about day 25. Fixed in `80c6aa5` by porting DEPONS's `Ship.move()`: one
waypoint per tick, waits honoured, route restarted.

**Run 2, same bands, same Java runs, CENOP rerun with the fix:**

| Metric | Run 1 | Run 2 | DEPONS |
|---|---|---|---|
| Share of animal-ticks with ship deterrence | 0.021 | 0.283 | 0.192 |
| N with ships / N undisturbed, day 359 | 0.994 | 0.911 | 0.918 |
| Population, day 90 | 1291.5 | 1285.9 | 1314.9 |
| Population, day 359 | 89.4 | 81.9 | 85.2 |
| Mean energy, day 30 | 7.86 | 7.86 | 7.98 |

Run 2 is not blind: the Java results were known when the fix was made. The bands were not
changed.

**What is still different.** The exposure ratio is about 1.45 throughout the year, so it is
not a matter of timing. In DEPONS's tracked animals a ship is audible (received level above
the reaction threshold) on 35.7% of animal-ticks and the animal reacts on 19.3%. Checked and
ruled out as the main cause: day/night handling (CENOP uses a fixed 6-18 h day; with the
landscape's sunrise table its exposure over the first 20 days is 0.249 instead of 0.260,
against about 0.16 in DEPONS). Not yet checked: where the sound propagation reads depth,
sediment and salinity (DEPONS at the ship's position, CENOP at the animal's), and the
reaction probability and its random draws per sub-step.

## What was compared

- **Models.** DEPONS 3.2, the unmodified release, run headless through the Track A1 harness
  (`validation/depons_java`); CENOP at commit `80c6aa5`, default NumPy/Numba backend.
- **Landscape.** Kattegat, DEPONS v3.2 (CENOP's `data/Kattegat` is byte-identical to the
  files DEPONS downloads).
- **Runs.** 2000 animals, seeds 42-49 on each side. The two models use different random
  number streams, so a seed is a replicate, not a matched pair; only distributions over seeds
  are compared.
- **Scenarios.** Three undisturbed (InnerDanishWaters, the mode DEPONS prescribes for
  Kattegat; PSM-Type2; dispersal off) and one disturbed (ship traffic from the v3.2 ship
  file, 5,148 ships, InnerDanishWaters). Scenario files: `validation/a2/scenarios/`.
- **Day convention.** Day d is the end of day d on both sides.

## Metrics and bands

Fixed on 2026-11-19 in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-a2-cross-validation-design.md` and
coded in `validation/a2/metrics.py` before the runs finished. J and C are means over seeds;
SE is the standard error of their difference.

| # | Metric | Band |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | Population at checkpoints | within 2 SE and within 10% of J |
| M2 | Population trajectory | C inside J +/- 2 SD(J) on at least 95% of days |
| M3 | Mean energy, population-wide | within 2 SE and within 0.25 |
| M4 | Share of animals per 40 km block, days 30-360 | total variation distance at most 0.06, correlation at least 0.98 |
| M5 | Age at death | two-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov test on pooled ages, p above 0.01 |
| M6 | Ship effect: N with ships / N undisturbed at day 359 | within 2 SE |
| M7 | Share of animal-ticks with ship deterrence | within 20% of J |

A pass is reported as inconclusive when 2 SE is larger than the band, that is, when the runs
could not have shown a difference of the forbidden size.

## How blind the comparison was

- **Undisturbed scenarios: not blind.** They were compared repeatedly on 2026-11-18/19 while
  CENOP was being corrected, before any band existed. They are reported with the bands
  applied afterwards and should be read as a record of agreement, not as a test passed.
- **Ship scenario: blind except for one seed.** Before the bands were fixed, one seed per
  side had been seen for population (four days), and two rough exposure numbers (one Java
  tracked animal; a two-day CENOP smoke test). No multi-seed result and no energy, location
  or age-at-death number for this scenario had been seen. The analysis code was committed
  before the runs finished.

## Limits

- **One landscape.** DEPONS reference runs exist only for its own landscapes. Nothing here
  validates CENOP on Lithuania or CentralBaltic, whose food layers are placeholders.
- **One backend.** The NumPy/Numba path. The JAX path cannot run InnerDanishWaters, and the
  fused Cython path is used on homogeneous landscapes only.
- **No turbines.** DEPONS ships no Kattegat turbine scenario.
- **Ship reactions are statistically, not draw-for-draw, equivalent.** CENOP draws each
  ship's reaction numbers from its own stream; only rates can match.
- **Seed spread is large.** With 2000 starting animals, year-end populations differ between
  seeds by 3-8 animals (SD). Two false alarms on 2026-11-19 came from comparing against two
  Java seeds, and a third from comparing a population mean with the mean of 200 tracked
  animals. Eight seeds per side and population-wide quantities are the minimum for this
  scenario.
- **Mode of comparison.** Only aggregate distributions are compared. Individual trajectories
  cannot be, because the random streams differ.
