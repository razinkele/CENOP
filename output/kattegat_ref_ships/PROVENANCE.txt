SUPERSEDES the prior baseline (commit eca6f96, ship-deterrence sub-tick
interpolation). This regeneration folds in the 2026-07-06 deep-review DEPONS-parity
fixes that change default-path model behaviour and/or the seeded RNG stream:

  - Paused / no-buoy ships are now excluded from the deterrence candidate set
    (DEPONS Ship.deterPorpoise gate: current_buoy_idx >= 0 and ticks_paused <= 0).
    A paused ship previously kept deterring (its 30 interpolated sub-positions
    collapse to a stationary point) for the whole pause — the direct cause of the
    small reduction in deterrence events below.
  - Food regrows once per day (was twice) on the vectorized path.
  - PersistentSpatialMemory uses the population's seeded RNG and centres
    preferred_distance on params.psm_dist_mean (350, DEPONS 3.2) — this inserts
    per-agent normal draws into the stream and shifts ALL downstream seeded
    outputs (positions, headings, mating days, dispersal targets).
  - Weaned calves that recycle a dead agent's slot are fully reset to newborn
    state (SoA fields + PSM preferred_distance/memory + behaviour/energy rows + id),
    no longer inheriting the dead occupant's state.
  - Per-tick birth/death and per-cause mortality (deaths_starvation/old_age/bycatch)
    are now true counts written to Population.txt (previously the per-cause columns
    were always 0); they reset monthly in step with births/deaths.

Run summary:
  - 2 yr (34,560 ticks), N=2000, population stable (2000 -> 1998; min 1991, max 2000)
  - 183,039 nonzero deter_strength records, max 0.3163
    (compare: prior sub-tick baseline had 188,072 nonzero, max 0.3168 -> ~2.7% fewer
     events from excluding paused ships during their pause; peak essentially unchanged)
  - Population stability confirms the changes are not pathological over/under-deterrence.
